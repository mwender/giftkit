Three short pages for donors

  1. The gift

    One-time or monthly, a preset amount or their own, an optional note, and the option to cover the processing fee.

  2. The donor

    Name, email, phone and billing address, with a summary of their gift and a way back to change it.

  3. Payment

    Card payment through Stripe, and a total that says whether it's charged once or every month.

An ordinary Gravity Forms form underneath

GiftKit builds the form you'd otherwise assemble by hand: three pages, the pricing fields, conditional logic, a one-time and a monthly Stripe feed, a thank-you message, a thank-you email for donors and an admin notification.

Entries, notifications and your other add-ons work as they always have. Edit any field you like: GiftKit finds the ones it manages by their role, not their position, so its settings keep working.

What you need

  • Gravity Forms 2.9 or later
  • The Gravity Forms Stripe Add-On, connected to your Stripe account
  • WordPress 6.5 or later, and PHP 8.1 or later
